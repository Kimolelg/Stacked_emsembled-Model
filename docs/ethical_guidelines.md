# Ethical Guidelines for the Teacher Mental Health Risk Prediction Tool

**Version:** Week 1-2 Deliverable  
**Date:** July 2026

## Core Principles

1. **Do No Harm**  
   This tool must **never** be used to stigmatize, punish, demote, or disadvantage any teacher.

2. **Screening, Not Diagnosis**  
   The model outputs a statistical risk probability based on self-reported survey patterns. It is **not** a clinical diagnosis.

3. **Human Oversight is Mandatory**  
   Every high-risk prediction must be reviewed by qualified mental health professionals or trained institutional support staff before any action is taken.

4. **Transparency & Explainability**  
   Feature contributions (via SHAP in future iterations) and clear probability scores must be available.

5. **Data Privacy & Consent**  
   All data used for training and inference must be anonymized. Informed consent and clear data retention policies are required.

6. **Equity & Bias Mitigation**  
   Ongoing monitoring for disparate impact across gender, age, school type, and location is essential.

## Recommended Use Cases (Institutional Level)

- Early identification for **voluntary** wellness program outreach
- Resource allocation for school counseling services
- Institutional mental health needs assessment and planning
- Research and policy development (aggregated, anonymized)

## Prohibited Uses

- Individual performance evaluation or promotion decisions
- Disciplinary or termination processes
- Public shaming or labeling of teachers
- Insurance or benefits discrimination
- Any automated decision-making without human review

## Response Protocol for High-Risk Flags (Recommended)

1. **Immediate:** Supportive, non-judgmental outreach by trained personnel
2. **Short-term:** Offer confidential counseling / EAP referral
3. **Medium-term:** Workload review + peer support options
4. **Long-term:** Systemic interventions (policy, training, culture change)

## Disclaimer Text (Mandatory in all interfaces)

> "This is an AI screening support tool only. It is NOT a medical diagnosis. A high-risk result indicates elevated symptoms consistent with moderate-to-severe depression based on historical patterns and should prompt professional support. Please consult a qualified mental health professional. If you are in crisis, contact emergency services or a helpline immediately."
